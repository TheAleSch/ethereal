# Demo audio

`beethoven-symphony-1-mvt1.mp3` — Ludwig van Beethoven, Symphony No. 1 in C
(Op. 21), I. Adagio molto — Allegro con brio. Performed by the Marine Chamber
Orchestra of the **United States Marine Band**, 27 January 2019.

**Public domain, twice over.** The composition (1800) is long out of copyright,
and the recording is a work of the United States federal government, which by
17 U.S.C. § 105 carries no copyright of its own. That second half is the part
that matters: most "free" classical recordings are PD *compositions* performed
under a fresh performance copyright. This one is not.

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Symphony_No._1_in_C_-_I._Adagio_molto,_Allegro_con_brio_-_Chamber_Orchestra_-_United_States_Marine_Band.opus
- Performer: https://www.marineband.marines.mil/

This file is a 34-second excerpt (from 8:32), loudness-normalized to -18 LUFS
with short fades, mono, 96 kbps. The excerpt was chosen for its DYNAMICS rather
than its tune: it runs loud, drops to near-silence, then hits a fortissimo — an
audio-reactive effect driven by a compressed, uniformly loud track looks
broken, because nothing about it ever changes.

It ships with the SITE, not with the npm package.
